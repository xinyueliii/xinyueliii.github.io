Xinyue Li is an undergraduate student at the School of Computer Science and Technology, Tongji University, Shanghai, China, from 2022 to 2026. She is engaged in research under the supervision of Professor Zhangkai Ni. Her research interests include Computer Vision, Image and Video Processing, Computer Graphics, and Biomedical Image Analysis, with a primary focus on Generative Modeling, Neural Rendering, 3D Reconstruction, Image and Video Restoration, Perceptual Analysis of Images and Videos, and Biomedical Image Analysis.

Currently, she is applying for her master's/Ph.D. degree. She is highly interested in the fields of embodied AI, reinforcement learning, large language models (LLMs), natural language processing (NLP), and multimodal research.
